Phonon 31m English model and default voice: Apache-2.0
https://huggingface.co/gradium/phonon-31m-en/tree/8c64b55ec301728e2d1695bfea87a08a267d0c2d
The model, tokenizer, configuration and default voice are downloaded once from
this pinned revision. Speech is generated locally in a browser worker.
No text or audio is sent to a speech service.

Installed browser/OS voices are provided by the player's operating system and
are not redistributed by this application. Only local device voices are used
when the player explicitly selects the device-voice alternative.
